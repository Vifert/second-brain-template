## 1.0.0

The first public release: the vault architecture, its tooling and 89 guarded
defects, a `CLAUDE.md` split into an always-on core and path-scoped rules,
hooks for three recurring traps, optional graph colours, a tailoring interview
that fits the vault to each user's own use, the gated compile, audit and
handoff skills, the Excalidraw drawing tools, the
setup guide for Claude Code, and the shared benchmark with its first
scorecard.
