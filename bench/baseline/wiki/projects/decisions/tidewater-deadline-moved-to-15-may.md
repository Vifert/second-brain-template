## Key Takeaways

- **Tidewater's first version was due on 30 April 2026 and the deadline moved to 15 May**, decided at the status meeting on 14 April 2026.
- **The reason is the KPI list growing from twelve to seventeen** after the mid-April ops review, which needed new columns and a second pass through the dashboard layout.
- **The arithmetic**: twelve agreed at the kickoff, two merged into one on 9 April, six added by the ops review — which is how twelve became seventeen.
- **Growth in the KPI list was a named risk at the kickoff**, so the move was a foreseen cost rather than a surprise.
- **[[mira-identity|Mira Okafor]] predicted it before the review**: on 9 April 2026, where [[sam-achebe|Sam Achebe]] read [[hannah-voss|Hannah Voss]]'s "a few more" KPIs as five, her note reads "I think that means the deadline".
- **Staging on 21 April 2026 left room before the new date**, with production waiting on a week of ops-team use.

## The decision

The deadline for Tidewater's first live version moves from 30 April to
**15 May 2026**. Agreed at the status meeting on 14 April 2026 by
[[mira-identity|Mira Okafor]], [[tomas-lind|Tomas Lind]] and
[[sam-achebe|Sam Achebe]].

## Why

The ops review added six KPIs and merged two. The extra KPIs need new columns and
a second pass through the dashboard layout, and that is what the fortnight pays
for.

## How twelve became seventeen

| When | The list |
| --- | --- |
| 2026-03-10 | Twelve agreed at the kickoff, as [[sam-achebe|Sam Achebe]]'s action |
| 2026-03-16 | Eleven drafted, one short of the twelve |
| 2026-03-16 | Two of the eleven — "on-time delivery rate" and "late shipments this week" — spotted as the same thing measured twice |
| 2026-04-09 | Those two merged into one KPI with a weekly and a monthly view, signed off by [[hannah-voss|Hannah Voss]] |
| mid-April | The ops review added six |
| 2026-04-14 | Seventeen |

## Foreseen, not a surprise

"The ops team may ask for more KPIs once they see the first ones" was written down
as a kickoff risk on 10 March 2026. [[hannah-voss|Hannah Voss]] had hinted at "a
few more" before the review.

## What Mira took from it

Her reflection on 21 April 2026, seven weeks from kickoff to staging: the parts
that went fast were the ones written down early — the storage choice, the "late"
definition, the refresh schedule. The part that went slow was the KPI list, which
nobody wrote down until the ops review forced it.
