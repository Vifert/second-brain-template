## Key Takeaways

- **Mira Okafor was a junior analyst at Quayside Logistics from 2021 to 2022**, her first role after the MSc.
- **Mira Okafor did the weekly reporting on delivery performance there**, and moved those reports from spreadsheets to SQL.
- **Mira Okafor built Quayside's first carrier scorecard**, which was used in its quarterly carrier reviews.
- **The carrier scorecard is also where Mira Okafor learned what not to do**: she built for three weeks before asking anyone what they wanted, and rebuilt half of it.
- **The Quayside scorecard lesson shaped how Tidewater was run** — talk to the ops lead first, write decisions down as they happen, and record why rather than only what.

## The role

Junior analyst, 2021–2022. As the CV puts it: weekly reporting on delivery
performance, moved from spreadsheets to SQL; and the first carrier scorecard, used
in quarterly carrier reviews.

## The lesson from the carrier scorecard

Written in the journal on 2 March 2026, at the start of Tidewater:

> Starting a project from nothing is the part I like best and do worst. Last time
> (the carrier scorecard at Quayside) I built for three weeks before asking anyone
> what they wanted, and rebuilt half of it.

The three rules she set for Tidewater in the same entry came directly from it:
talk to [[hannah-voss|Hannah Voss]] first; write decisions down as they happen;
keep a note of why, not just what — so that "why did we do X?" can be answered in
six months without digging through chat history.

## Why it matters to this vault

That last sentence is a statement of what a second brain is for, written before
this vault existed, and the reason Tidewater's decisions each have their own node.
