## The place, as the work describes it

- **The ops floor** is about fifteen people, busiest in the morning, filtering by
  carrier, region, customer, week and status.
- **Three warehouses**, whose fill [[driftwood-capacity-forecast|Driftwood]]
  forecasts six weeks ahead.
- **The platform team** defaults new services to DynamoDB, already runs managed
  Postgres for two other services, and reviews its choices quarterly.
- **Finance** drops a nightly invoices CSV, any time between 23:00 and 01:30, and
  sometimes reissues an invoice under a new number.
- **Carriers** are reached through an API that pages at 200 records and
  rate-limits after about 40 calls a minute.
