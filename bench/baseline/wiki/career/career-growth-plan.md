## The goal

To lead a project's design from the start rather than only building what has
already been designed. Said in the 1:1 of 26 March 2026.
[[tomas-lind|Tomas Lind]]'s response: [[tidewater-dashboard|Tidewater]] is that
project, if the decisions keep being written down. His later reading, recorded the
same day, was that leading a project from the start is mostly writing things down
early and letting people disagree with the written version.

By 21 April 2026 Mira Okafor's own verdict on that method was that the parts of
Tidewater which went fast were exactly the ones written down early — the storage
choice, the "late" definition, the refresh schedule — and the part that went slow
was the KPI list, which nobody wrote down until the ops review forced it.
