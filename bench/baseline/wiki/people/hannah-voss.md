## Key Takeaways

- **Hannah Voss is the ops lead at Harbor Analytics** and runs the ops floor of about fifteen people that Tidewater is built for.
- **Hannah Voss chose Tidewater's definition of "late"** at the kickoff on 10 March 2026: delivered after the date promised to the customer. Finance's meaning, invoiced after delivery, became a separate KPI called "slow invoicing".
- **Hannah Voss trusted exactly one tab of the 23-tab ops spreadsheet** — the one called "Hannah's view" — which is why Mira Okafor spoke to her before the kickoff rather than after.
- **Hannah Voss asked for "live" numbers at the ops review** and, asked what she would do differently with numbers from a minute ago rather than a quarter of an hour ago, said nothing. That answer settled the 15-minute refresh.
- **Hannah Voss's reaction to the staging deploy on 21 April 2026**, by message, was "finally".

## How she comes up

The ops lead Tidewater serves. She attended the first half of the kickoff on
10 March 2026, and the mid-April ops review.

## Her four questions, as she said them at the kickoff

1. "Which shipments are late right now, and whose fault is it?"
2. "Which customers owe us the most, and for how long?"
3. "Which carrier should I shout at this week?"
4. "Stop me having to update the spreadsheet on Mondays."

Everything else she raised at the kickoff was a variation of those four, and
[[sam-achebe|Sam Achebe]] turned them into the first KPIs.
