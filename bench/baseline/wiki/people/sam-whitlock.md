## Key Takeaways

- **Sam Whitlock has the allotment plot next to Mira Okafor's** and has been there thirty years, with opinions about everything, including her potatoes.
- **Sam Whitlock's watering test is a finger in the soil to the second knuckle**: if it is dry there, water. That test is the reference the soil-moisture sensor calibrates against.
- **Sam Whitlock laughed at the idea of a soil sensor** on 26 March 2026. Mira Okafor's note: "I'm going to build one anyway."
- **Sam Whitlock's potato advice is to earth them up as soon as the shoots show** (16 April 2026).
- **Sam Whitlock is not the Sam on Tidewater** — that is Sam Achebe, the analyst.
