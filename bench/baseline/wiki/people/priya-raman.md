## Key Takeaways

- **Priya Raman is Mira Okafor's co-author** on *Sparse and Hybrid Retrieval for Personal Field Notes*, and second author of the three.
- **Priya Raman owns the fix to the paper's dataset description** — the abstract and the results table do not give the same number of notes, which Tomas Lind flagged on 26 March 2026.
- **Priya Raman's framing is that personal notes are short, private and untitled**, so web retrieval results may not carry over — the argument the paper opens with.
- **Priya Raman gave the Leeds Data Meetup talk on 25 April 2026**, moved from 18 April because the venue was double-booked; the hybrid results drew the most questions.
