## How This Page Works

- **Generated by `tools/build-index.js` on every build. Never edit it by hand** — edits are overwritten.
- An item appears here when its node has a `## Status Log` and an open `status` (active, pending, waiting, blocked, in-progress, exploring, seed). Closing the status (done, completed, cancelled, parked, superseded) drops it off this page; its Status Log stays as history.
- To change a status: append a dated row to the owner's Status Log, update its one `**Status` takeaway, set `status:`, rebuild.
